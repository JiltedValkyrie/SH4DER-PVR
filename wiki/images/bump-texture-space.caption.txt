The bump comparator in texture space (recipes R7 and R8, not built): when M is a texture in the object's own u, v (a height or a noise) and Q is one value for the whole object (a water level, a dissolve time), the bump unit's window passes exactly the pixels with |M - Q| <= w, with no bands, no camera depth map and no screen coordinates; the mechanism is MEASURED (PVR-045, PVR-065), the window edges must come from boot measurements (PVR-096, Q56), and the recipes are ESTIMATE.
