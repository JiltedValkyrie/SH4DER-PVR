The placing trap: the SH-4 operand cache is 16 KB, direct-mapped, indexed by address bits 13..5, so two arrays a multiple of 16 KB apart put line i of each in the same entry and every access misses; measured on one console, mat_transform 13.16 against 38.67 cycles a vertex (1 MB apart), pvr_project 21.15 against 132.5 (256 KB apart in c4), memcpy 398.0 against 46.6 MB/s; the fix is one pool with the second array 8 KB off modulo 16 KB (SH4-002, 003, 020-023, 030-032, 046).
