Why KallistiOS's ns clock jumps: its timer TMU2 reloads every 12,468,720 counts of 80 ns, 997,497,600 ns, and KallistiOS then adds a whole second, so the ns clock steps ahead 2,502,400 ns once a reload (FACT, the KallistiOS source); ray2's CLOCK.4 measured the step at 2,502,321 ns, one 80 ns count short, and 5,070 us gained over 2.2 s, two steps plus 30 ppm (MEASURED, one console, SH4-057, SH4-058); any interval timed with timer_ns_gettime64 across a reload reads 2.5 ms long, which fits ray1's two odd rows, so benches time with the cycle counter.
