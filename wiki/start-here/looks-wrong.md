# When it looks wrong: symptom, cause, fix

**What you will learn:** how to find a picture problem from what you see: the likely cause, the fix, and the page or header that says so.
**For:** anyone whose effect runs but looks wrong, on the host picture or on the console.

Each row gives its source. Where the sources do not say something, the row says **Not in the sources:** and names the gap. A gap means nobody has written it down yet. It does not mean the fix cannot be done. Most effects have no console number yet, so check the status before you trust a row ([What do you want to make?](what-do-you-want.md)).

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Planar reflection [pl4nar]: the picture is upright, empty or missing.** | Upright: no mirror. Empty: the mirror pass is culled the wrong way round. A zero from the target init or the view build draws no layer, and the screen shows no error. | The world must show upside down, horizon at the top. Upright: put the mirror in. Empty: reverse the cull in the mirror pass. Test each return value. Build the view rows directly; do not chain matrix calls. | AGENTS.md, "pl4nar in an engine that has no normals", step 1; `wiki/effects/pl4nar.md:66-71`; `include/sh4der_pl4nar.h:287` (view build: 0 means draw no layer), `:489` (target init: 0 for no memory). **Not in the sources:** how to see a silent zero on screen. |
| **Planar reflection: the picture sits in the left part of a wider target.** | `SH4DER_PL4NAR_HAVE_RTT` is not set, or the KallistiOS has no `pvr_scene_begin_rtt`. The target is then 1,024 pixels wide. | Set the flag where `pvr_scene_begin_rtt` exists. | `include/sh4der_pl4nar.h:473-475`; `wiki/effects/pl4nar.md:69` |
| **Light pools [pool]: the pool flickers, or it covers the car.** | A fan lying on the road with no lift fights the road. A fan drawn before the road, or one that writes depth, covers things. A fan seen edge-on has no screen area and drops out. | Lift the centre 2 to 4 cm along the normal. Draw after the road. Write no depth. Test each triangle with `sh4der_tri_area_ok`. | `include/sh4der_pool.h:9`, `:17`; `wiki/effects/pool.md:61`, `:64`. **Not in the sources:** why a pool covers a car. The [racing guide](../guides/racing-game.md), section 5 (line 419), says that cause is not known yet. |
| **Light pool or beam pops in at the edge of its range.** | The street demo fades its lamps by distance. The fade is a PROTOTYPE, run on a console by the owner in Street car demo (her test; no measured numbers recorded yet). | Call `sh4der_pool_reach_make` once a frame, then `sh4der_pool_reach_fade` for each lamp. Skip a lamp whose fade is 0. Start values: reach 60 m, fade width 10 m (ADVISED). | `include/sh4der_pool.h:20-33`, `:100-101` (the status is at `:28-29`). **Not in the sources:** the console look and cost of the fade. |
| **A shadow pops on or off as its caster moves.** | A hard range cut with no fade. A tile is released at the range, not at zero strength. | Give every shadow a maximum distance. Fade its strength to 0 before that distance. Release a tile when its strength reaches 0. | `wiki/limits/frame-budget.md:117`. **Not in the sources:** the 40 m and 50 m values are chosen, not measured. |
| **A shadow is too dark, too faint, or darker on one side.** | (a) A caster that is not closed, or is wound both ways, darkens one side wrongly. (b) The shadow-map route has no strength setting: `sh4der_psm_raster_lit` returns 1 for lit and 0 for shadowed, and your code sets how dark that is. | Check each caster's winding and that it is closed. Set the darkness in your own code. | `wiki/effects/aosky.md:70`; `include/sh4der_psm_raster.h:372-375`. **Not in the sources:** a value that gives the right strength. |
| **A glow ends in a hard line where it meets a wall.** | The library reads no depth. Soft edges fade against the planes and rectangles you name, and not against a car or a curved shape. | Name the wall with `sh4der_softfade_wall` (the plane route). A camera depth map is not in the library. | `wiki/effects/softfade.md:59`, `:67`; `include/sh4der_softfade.h:21`; README, "The softfade effect". **Not in the sources:** a console cost for the plane route. The depth route ran in a test program: the hard line went (MEASURED, PVR-204), and its map cost 5.1 ms of PVR (MEASURED, PVR-203, a test program's own code). |
| **The HUD or an inset is missing, and the end of the picture is cut.** | The TA vertex buffer is full. The end of the list is dropped with no message. KallistiOS does not report these events. | Send the HUD early, or give it its own room. Watch the buffer's peak. | `wiki/limits/frame-budget.md:115`; `wiki/limits/recommended-limits.md:85` (errata K7). **Not in the sources:** that a full buffer drops the HUD is an ESTIMATE from PVR-149 (`wiki/hardware/ledger-pvr.md:219`). PVR-185 records it in one run (MEASURED). |
| **The frame freezes on a bigger scene.** | Likely: the vertex buffer (1,024 KB) is too small for the largest scene (one pair of runs; the cause is not proven). | Size the buffer from the largest scene (`vtx_buffer_used_max`): 1,536 KB where a scene reaches 1 MB. Two buffers at 1,536 KB take 1,024 KB more video memory than two at 1,024 KB (ESTIMATE, arithmetic). | `wiki/limits/frame-budget.md:179`; MEASURED, PVR-208 and PVR-209 (one pair of runs). The sources disagree on the count: frame-budget.md:179 says the same ten turns froze; PVR-208 says the second turn froze after the first ran whole. **Not in the sources:** why it froze. The mechanism is OPEN. |
| **The blur or glow has a grey cast, or the exposure drifts.** | The log and exp in the exposure code are short series. SH4ZAM's fast forms read log(1) as 0.040 and exp(-0.012) as 0.963, which gives the grey cast. | Check a still frame with the eye held. Give the step a read with the gain taken off. | `wiki/effects/look.md:58-60` (which cites README:415). **Not in the sources:** a fix to the series itself. |
| **A glow keeps brightening, feeds itself, or makes the HUD glow.** | The glow reads its own glow and gain back from the frame. A HUD brighter than the threshold glows like a lamp. | Take the glow and the gain off the read with `sh4der_glow_unfeed` and `sh4der_frame_degain`. Keep the HUD under the threshold, or draw it after the glow. | `wiki/effects/glow.md:57-58` |
| **Sparks are missing when many fly.** | The pool is full of KEEP quads. A plain quad is then dropped, and counted. | Give KEEP to what must never be pushed out, such as a flash or a tracer. Read `dropped` in the pool. | `include/sh4der_spark.h:50-54`, `:111`, `:131` |
| **Sparks sit far behind the near geometry.** | The screen record's depth fields do not match your world's depth key. | Build the record with `sh4der_spark_screen_make`, or set `z_base` and `z_scale` to match your key. | `include/sh4der_spark.h:392-396`, `:405`, `:413-419`; `wiki/effects/spark.md:58`. **Not in the sources:** no real conflict. The spark page (`wiki/effects/spark.md:58`) says a record built field by field must set both depth fields; the header (`sh4der_spark.h:400-401`) and README "The spark effect" (`README.md:929`) say the same, and add that a zero `z_scale` reads as 1. Not DISPUTED. The header's cost line says "not measured" (`sh4der_spark.h:13`). |
| **A bright line shows where a fan or triangle should be nothing.** | A triangle with no screen area. A real console draws it as a bright line. An emulator draws nothing. | Test each triangle with `sh4der_tri_area_ok` before you send it. | `wiki/effects/guard.md:25`; `wiki/limits/recommended-limits.md:23` |
| **Nothing draws, and the console stops.** | A polygon header was never compiled. Its command word is 0, which means end of list. An emulator carries on. | Test every header with `sh4der_poly_hdr_ok`. | `wiki/effects/guard.md:26`; `include/sh4der_guard.h:98` |
| **The glowing cable is dark, or every cable pulses in step.** | Bad parameters make `sh4der_blade_prepare` return 0 and leave a dark frame. All objects share one phase. | Check `sh4der_blade_params_valid` at load, and the prepare return each frame. Give each object its own phase: tick plus 57 × its index. | `wiki/effects/blade.md:50-51`; `include/sh4der_blade.h:141`, `:146` |
| **A colour fringe does nothing, and there is no error.** | The setup could not get video memory. It returns 0 and gives `short_kb`, and the effect is off. | Print `short_kb` once. Check free video memory with `pvr_mem_available()` (a KallistiOS call; `demo/main.c:914` uses it) after load. | `wiki/effects/ca.md:46`; `wiki/limits/frame-budget.md:116` |

## If none of the rows fits

Turn the effect off, then turn on one part at a time, so the part that breaks the picture shows itself. Check the status line of the effect's page: a PROTOTYPE or NOT RUN effect has no console result to compare against. Then read the [send checks](../effects/guard.md) page, because many "nothing draws" faults come from a triangle or a header.
