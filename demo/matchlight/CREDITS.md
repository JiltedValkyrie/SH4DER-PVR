Nothing listed here is in the repository: `tools/audio.py --fetch` downloads each file and checks its sha256
(`audio_public.json`). Every sound file is CC0 1.0 on its page; CC0 needs no credit, and we credit anyway. The font is under the SIL Open Font License.

Narrator: the film maker's own recording, supplied at build time; not in the repository.

which uses neither font's name) and the licence texts (`LICENSES/`).

Subtitle type: Aldrich, Copyright (c) Matthew Desmond, 2011, SIL Open Font License 1.1. The font file is fetched at build time

Narration written for this film by the project, in its own words. Tale: Hans Christian Andersen, "Den lille Pige med
Svovlstikkerne" (first published December 1845, Danish; public domain). Beats checked against "The Fairy Tales of Hans
Christian Andersen" (Philadelphia: J. B. Lippincott, 1899; translator not named; public domain by 1899 publication in the
US; archive.org item fairytalesofhans00ande, retrieved 2026-11-23; sha256 pins in `audio_public.json`, "references"). No
translated text is quoted or shipped.
